⚡ Pocket Scratch & Field Check

  • Pocket Knife / Steel Nail (Mohs ~5.5): Won't scratch Agate, Jasper, or Quartzite (Mohs 7). Will scratch Petoskey stones (Mohs 4.5).
  • Water Test: Wet the rock! Agate bands and Petoskey coral patterns pop brightly when wet.
  • 365nm UV Light: Best wavelength for spotting Yooperlite glowing orange embers.
