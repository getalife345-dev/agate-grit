⚡ Pocket Scratch & Field Check

  • Pocket Knife / Steel Nail (Mohs ~5.5): Won't scratch Agate, Jasper, Chert, or Quartzite (Mohs 7).
  • Water Test: Wet the stone! Agate banding and Unakite colors pop immediately when wet.
  • 365nm UV Light: Best wavelength for spotting glowing orange Yooperlite veins.
